General project questions may use GitHub discussions or issues once those

## Succession and inactivity

Additional maintainers may be appointed under `GOVERNANCE.md`. If the project
lead becomes unavailable and no successor has been appointed, the repository
should remain available under its existing license but no new release should
claim maintainer approval until a successor is publicly documented.
