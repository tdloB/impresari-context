# Future Commercialization Backlog

## Document Control

- Product: Impresari Context and the Impresari portal.
- Status: Exploration backlog; not an approved roadmap commitment.
- Recorded: 2026-08-22.
- Review trigger: After the open-source MVP is complete, evaluated, and publicly released with evidence of adoption or organizational demand.

## Idea IC-COM-001 — Open Local Evidence Engine With Managed Team Infrastructure

### Hypothesis

Keep the local Impresari Context evidence engine fully open source and independently useful. Explore paid, additive services for organizations that need shared infrastructure, governance, operations, and support. The Impresari portal would provide the managed coordination and operational experience without weakening or feature-gating the local open-source core.

### Potential paid capabilities

- Managed evidence infrastructure shared across teams and agents.
- Hosted packet storage, synchronization, retention, and recovery.
- Managed repository connectors and incremental synchronization.
- Central policy administration and organizational context profiles.
- Long-term audit retention, evidence search, and organizational dashboards.
- Enterprise deployment and security integration.
- Guided onboarding, implementation services, priority support, and SLAs.

### Principles to evaluate

- Local evidence construction remains useful without an account or hosted service.
- Commercial capabilities are additive hosting, coordination, governance, scale, and support—not removal of local functionality.
- Customers retain control of repository access, evidence policy, retention, and deletion.
- Hosted services must not weaken the core's deterministic, source-verifiable, capability-bounded contracts.
- Open-source and commercial boundaries must be documented before implementation.

### Questions for later research

1. Which organizational problems produce demonstrated willingness to pay?
2. Which capabilities belong in the OSS core, portal, managed service, or optional self-hosted control plane?
3. What repository and packet data would be stored, where, for how long, and under whose encryption keys?
4. What pricing basis best aligns with value: seat, workspace, storage, usage, support, or a hybrid?
5. Which features require enterprise identity, audit, compliance, data-residency, or SLA commitments?
6. How will hosted services interoperate with third-party orchestrators without forcing adoption of the Impresari portal?
7. What governance prevents commercial priorities from degrading the open-source core?

### Evidence required before promotion to roadmap

- Completed and evaluated open-source MVP.
- Public adoption or qualified customer-discovery evidence.
- Verified demand for cross-device, team, governance, hosted, or support capabilities.
- Architecture and threat-model review for hosted data and repository connectors.
- Competitive and pricing research using current primary sources.
- Explicit founder approval through the applicable OS planning and governance workflow.
