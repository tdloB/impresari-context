## Objective

Make Impresari Context compelling for larger organizations by admitting Java,
Kotlin, and C# and strengthening deterministic impact-oriented evidence.

## Scope

- Structural admissions for Java, Kotlin, and C#.
- Change-set context packets, bounded impact paths, associated-test evidence,
  repository-orientation packets, explicit incremental structural updates, and
  deterministic convention and exemplar evidence.

## Constraints

Each language and impact capability requires isolated parser, resolver,
evaluation, and authority-boundary evidence. No compiler, language server,
repository-code execution, or opaque semantic claim is implied.
